04 · When someone raises a hand

Every reply needs a next step.

A normal reply or a booking

Emails come from Brooks Kelly. Replies use Brooks’s branded reply address, with forwarding configured for bkteam@planethomelending.com.

The team handles questions and arranges Scott’s first conversation. A reply or booking does not automatically remove the person from nurture.

Before launch: test actual inbox forwarding and confirm Scott’s booking is attached to the correct Brooks CRM contact.

A workbook keyword reply

  1. Recognize the keyword.
    PAYMENT, CASH, CREDIT, READY, COMPARE, SHOP, OFFER or PLAN.
  2. Send the matching workbook.
    Use its branded online page, with its explainer when available.
  3. Alert BK Team after provider acceptance.
    Tell the team which resource was requested and sent, and that someone should follow up.
  4. Make a personal follow-up.
    Scott and the team continue the conversation. This branch still needs a full live test before launch.

They use the workbook

They can type their answers, choose Send to Brooks for review, print or save the page, book a call or apply. These are planning exercises, not approval decisions.

The workbook’s Brooks booking link and the opener’s Scott booking link serve different steps.

They close—or opt out

Closed stays in. Closing or funding does not end this journey. Replies and bookings also keep the person in.

Opt-out stops outreach. Permission and suppression checks still apply. A closed record never overrides an unsubscribe.

06 · Before turning it on

The launch checklist.

Status checked November 10, 2026 at 1:53pm Central. This is a review snapshot, not a live dashboard.

  1. Review the remaining email copy. The flow is a draft with no enrolled contacts. Activation validation still reports 344 missing copy clearances; finish the actual content review before clearing them.
  2. Confirm permission and enrollment. Map documented email permission to the CRM without overriding opt-outs or suppression. Then enroll the eligible existing cohort and confirm how new eligible arrivals will enter; the current flow trigger is manual.
  3. Test replies and resource delivery. Send a controlled test through the real flow, reply normally and with a keyword, and verify the workbook email, BK Team forwarding/alert and follow-up handling.
  4. Finish booking attribution. Scott’s 30-minute calendar is available. Confirm a booking links back to the right contact in Brooks’s workspace.
  5. Check retargeting separately. The audience-sync ledger records 124 submitted identities. This is not proof of 124 matched people or of ads being served; confirm the live campaign’s audience and delivery.
  6. Activate, enroll and watch the first sends. Verify the sender, full signature, footer, opt-out behavior, timing and Scott’s tasks. Use the CRM flow builder to make subsequent changes.
